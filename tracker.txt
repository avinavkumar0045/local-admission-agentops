- Formulated and implemented novel continuous mathematical severity formula to solve the "Queue Inversion" problem in RAG operations:
  $$\Phi = \min(100, \; [0.40 \cdot \mathcal{I} + 0.60 \cdot \mathcal{S}] \times \mathcal{B} \times \mathcal{U})$$
  - $\mathcal{I}$ (Infrastructure Loss): Fatal crashes (100) or execution latency beyond 10s SLA target.
  - $\mathcal{S}$ (Semantic Grounding Loss): Weighted RAG Triad loss ($0.50(1-\mathcal{F}) + 0.30\mathbb{I}_{\text{hallucinated}} + 0.20(1-\mathcal{C}_{\text{rel}})$).
  - $\mathcal{B}$ (Domain Entity Liability): 1.5 for Statutory Risk (fees/refunds/approvals), 1.2 for Academic Eligibility, 0.8 for Campus Amenities.
  - $\mathcal{U}$ (Human Feedback Multiplier): 1.4 for student 👎 with notes, 1.2 for 👎 unannotated, 0.6 for 👍 (mitigates false alarms), 1.0 unrated.
- Dynamic Tier Classification: $\Phi \ge 75 \implies \text{P1 Critical}$, $40 \le \Phi < 75 \implies \text{P2 Warning}$, $\Phi < 40 \implies \text{P3 Info}$.
- Built dedicated engine in `src/agentops/priority_engine.py` and integrated into `alert_receiver.py`.
