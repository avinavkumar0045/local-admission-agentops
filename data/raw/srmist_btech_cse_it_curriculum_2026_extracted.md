B.Tech. - Computer Science and Engineering with Specialization in Information Technology/B.Tech. Information Technology
1. (a) Mission of the Department / School
Mission Stmt – 1 To impart knowledge in cutting edge Computer Science and Engineering technologies in par with industrial standards.
Mission Stmt – 2 To collaborate with renowned academic institutions to uplift innovative research and development in Computer Science
and Engineering and its allied fields to serve the needs of society
Mission Stmt – 3 To demonstrate strong communication skills and possess the ability to design computing systems individually as well
as part of a multidisciplinary team.
Mission Stmt – 4 To instill societal, safety, cultural, environmental, and ethical responsibilities in all professional activities
Mission Stmt – 5 To produce successful Computer Science and Engineering graduates with personal and professional responsibilities
and commitment
1. (b) Program Educational Objectives (PEO)
PEO – 1
Graduates will demonstrate professional competence by applying strong foundations in mathematics, basic sciences,
computing principles, algorithms, and modern technologies to analyze and solve complex engineering problems, and to
design and implement industry-standard solutions that address the evolving needs of software industries and R&D
sectors.
PEO – 2 Graduates will advance in their careers by adapting to the emerging trends of their professional pursuits such as
Industry, research, higher studies, and entrepreneurship
PEO – 3 Graduates will work effectively as individuals and as part of multi-disciplinary teams, exhibiting professional ethics,
strong communication skills, leadership qualities, and respect for diversity.
PEO – 4 Graduates will engage in lifelong learning to stay updated with technological advancements and contribute responsibly
to society by developing sustainable, secure, and human-centric computing solutions by adhering to societal, legal, and
environmental responsibilities.
PEO – 5 Graduates will demonstrate the ability to offer innovative cutting-edge solutions that meet the evolving industrial and
research challenges in the field of Information Technology.
