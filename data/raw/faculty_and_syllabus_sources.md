# Faculty and syllabus source catalog

These records expand the admissions corpus with faculty counts, department faculty listings, and semester-level engineering curricula.

## Important coverage distinction

AISHE is the India-wide source for aggregate teacher counts and distributions. Its published annual workbook does not provide a single public national roster of every teacher by name. Named faculty are available institution by institution through approved disclosures, annual handbooks, and faculty pages; these records can be stale and must carry institution, campus, department, and observation date.

AICTE model curricula provide national reference syllabi for common engineering disciplines. They are not a claim that all Indian colleges teach identical semester courses. University and autonomous-college curricula take precedence for a student-specific answer.

## Sources

- **AISHE Final Report 2023-24 (Excel).** National and state faculty counts, posts, gender and social-group distributions. Aggregated statistics; no nationwide person-level faculty directory. Source: https://aishe.gov.in/aishe-final-report/
- **AICTE Model Curriculum - Computer Science and Engineering.** India-wide model undergraduate engineering curriculum; semester structure and detailed course content. Institutions may adopt or modify it. Source: https://www.aicte.gov.in/sites/default/files/Updated-AICTE%20-%20UG%20CSE.pdf
- **AICTE Model Curriculum - Civil Engineering (January 2024).** India-wide model undergraduate Civil Engineering curriculum; semester structure and detailed course content. Source: https://www.aicte.gov.in/sites/default/files/AICTE%20Model%20Curriculum%20_UG_Civil_2024.pdf
- **AICTE Revised Model Curriculum - Electronics and Communication Engineering.** India-wide model undergraduate ECE curriculum; semester structure and detailed course content. Source: https://www.aicte.gov.in/sites/default/files/Final_ECE.pdf
- **AICTE Model Curriculum - Electrical Engineering.** India-wide model undergraduate Electrical Engineering curriculum; semester structure and detailed course content. Source: https://aicte.gov.in/sites/default/files/ug-vol1.pdf
- **AICTE Revised Model Curriculum - Mechanical Engineering.** India-wide model undergraduate Mechanical Engineering curriculum; semester structure and detailed course content. Source: https://www.aicte.gov.in/sites/default/files/Final_Mechanical%20Engg.pdf
- **SRMIST Engineering Handbook 2025-26.** Named faculty, qualifications, roles and academic departments by SRM campus. Handbook is a dated snapshot and not a live directory. Source: https://webstor.srmist.edu.in/web_assets/downloads/2025/hand-book-2025-26.pdf
- **SRMIST B.Tech CSE (Information Technology specialization) curriculum, Regulations 2026.** Eight-semester SRMIST curriculum, Kattankulathur. Specific to the CSE-IT specialization; not a universal SRM curriculum. Source: https://webstor.srmist.edu.in/web_assets/downloads/2026/srmist-btech-computer-science-and-engineering-information-technology-curriculum-2026.pdf
