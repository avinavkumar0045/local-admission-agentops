Eight institution groups: SRMIST, SRM University-AP, IIT Madras, IIT Delhi, NIT Tiruchirappalli, BITS Pilani, VIT and IIT Guwahati. Selected departments and campuses only.
SRMIST and SRM University-AP are separate institutions. SRMIST Delhi-NCR/Modinagar is not SRM University Delhi-NCR, Sonepat.
SRMIST non-Kattankulathur campuses have leadership extracts only in this release; their hostel details were not collected.
SRMIST medical-college hostel features apply only to the stated medical/health-science scope. Engineering room fees have their own programme and gender scope.
Missing fee tables, facilities, contacts or positions mean not collected/verified, not that the institution lacks them.
Current appointment start/end dates and live bed availability were not established. Dated snapshots must not be promoted to confirmed current appointments.
Amounts are INR. SRMIST annual room/mess schedules and NITT semester admission packages have different billing periods and inclusions.
PDFs here are newly prepared reference reports, not original university circulars. Source URLs identify the original official materials.
Source content was selected and paraphrased into factual records. No private contact data, student records or inferred reporting lines were added.
Conflicting roles are stored in quality_issues.json, outside the clean leadership records.
These files have not been ingested into the running vector database.
